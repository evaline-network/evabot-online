# Best Google Cloud TTS Voices

This document lists the most human-like, high-quality text-to-speech voices provided by Google Cloud for selected languages. The premium tiers include **Studio**, **Journey**, and **Neural2**, which offer exceptional naturalness and pronunciation. Where these are not yet supported, **WaveNet** is listed as the highest quality alternative.

## Pricing (per 1 Million Characters)
- **Studio:** $160.00
- **Neural2:** $16.00
- **WaveNet:** $16.00
- **Standard:** $4.00

*(Note: Prices are in USD and based on standard Google Cloud Text-to-Speech rates.)*

## Recommended Voices by Language

### English (EN)
English has the widest support for ultra-realistic voices.
- **Female:** `en-US-Studio-O`, `en-US-Journey-F`, `en-US-Neural2-C`
- **Male:** `en-US-Studio-M`, `en-US-Journey-D`, `en-US-Neural2-D`

### Russian (RU)
For the Russian language, the following high-quality models are recommended:
- **Female:** `ru-RU-Wavenet-A`, `ru-RU-Wavenet-C` (or Neural2 if available in your region)
- **Male:** `ru-RU-Wavenet-B`, `ru-RU-Wavenet-D`

### Ukrainian (UK)
For the Ukrainian language, WaveNet models provide the most natural output:
- **Female:** `uk-UA-Wavenet-A`
- **Male:** `uk-UA-Wavenet-M` (or standard WaveNet male equivalents)

### Polish (PL)
Polish supports advanced neural models for highly natural speech:
- **Female:** `pl-PL-Neural2-A`, `pl-PL-Wavenet-A`
- **Male:** `pl-PL-Neural2-B`, `pl-PL-Wavenet-B`

### Romanian (RO)
For the Romanian language, WaveNet offers the best available human-like quality:
- **Female:** `ro-RO-Wavenet-A`
- **Male:** `ro-RO-Wavenet-B`
