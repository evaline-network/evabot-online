## 2. ConsiliumEngine Architecture & Hardening

### 2.1 Mode Implementation Details

- **Solo Mode**:
  - Executes 1 user prompt against a chosen model (defaulting to `gemini-2.5-flash`).
  - Supports role assignment, custom temperature, and system prompt inheritance.
  - Hardened with `try/catch` block preventing upstream rate-limit (429) or provider failures from crashing the engine, returning a formatted error turn.

- **Broadcast Mode**:
  - Concurrent execution across $N$ models via `Promise.all`.
  - Emits real-time progress events (`round_complete`, `turn_start`, `turn_complete`).
  - Resilient to individual model failures: if one model throws an error or experiences network timeout, remaining models complete successfully and the failed turn documents the error message.

- **Dialogue Mode**:
  - Bilateral dialectic between 2 models over $K$ configurable rounds ($1 \le K \le 5$).
  - Automatic participant resolution: if only 1 participant is provided, automatically populates an opposing challenger role.
  - Turn-by-turn argument and critique exchange with context history accumulation.
  - Final Executive Synthesis produced by the Senior Technical Arbiter with consensus points, trade-offs, and definitive actionable recommendations with pricing impact in USD ($) or EUR (€).
  - Hardened with turn-level and synthesis-level error handling.

- **Consilium Mode**:
  - Multi-agent deliberation engine strictly bounded between **3 and 10 participants**.
  - **Round 1**: Concurrent independent vantage evaluations across all specialists.
  - **Rounds 2 to $K$**: Structured cross-evaluation and peer critiques referencing preceding arguments.
  - **Final Stage**: Authoritative Markdown Consensus Synthesis generated by the Supreme Technical Council Synthesizer structured into 5 standardized sections:
    1. Executive Summary & Final Verdict
    2. Unanimous Consensus & Strategic Alignment
    3. Disputed Decisions, Risk Analysis & Trade-Offs
    4. Implementation Roadmap & Technical Milestones
    5. Budgetary & Infrastructure Impact strictly calculated in USD ($) and EUR (€).
  - Hybrid Knowledge Base context retrieval and injection automatically included when enabled.

### 2.2 Participant Bounds & Parsing (`validateConsiliumParticipants`)

A dedicated public validator `validateConsiliumParticipants` was implemented:
- **$< 3$ participants (0, 1, or 2)**: Dynamically expands to at least 3 participants by assigning core corporate roles (`architect`, `devops`, `security_auditor`).
- **$3$ to $10$ participants**: Exact list preserved with resolved model identifiers and prompts.
- **$> 10$ participants (e.g., 11, 12, 15)**: Strictly clamped to the upper bound of 10 participants.
